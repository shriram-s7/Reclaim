1. Purpose and scope
This document describes the technical design of Reclaim: a mobile-ﬁrst web application that guides a victim of UPI or
banking fraud through the three oﬃcial reports — the 1930 helpline, a complaint on the National Cyber Crime
Reporting Portal (NCRP), and a written report to their bank — and tracks the deadlines that decide whether money
can be held and whether the victim qualiﬁes for compensation. A companion bank dashboard gives the bank's fraud
team a live view of incoming, complete cases.
It covers the hackathon prototype in detail and the intended production architecture at design level. Build sequencing
is in the separate Implementation Plan.
2. Problem and requirements
2.1 Problem
About 51% of UPI-fraud victims ﬁle no oﬃcial complaint; users describe reporting as complex (LocalCircles survey,
32,000+ responses, Jun 2025).
Recovery is low when reporting is late — Odisha recovered about 2.7% of ~₹370 crore lost in 2023–25, with police
citing delayed reporting.
Recovery needs three separate actions in three places; the 1930 call issues a 14-digit acknowledgement that must
then be used to complete the NCRP complaint.
From 1 Jan 2027, RBI's Third Amendment Directions (2026) compensate small-value frauds (loss ≤ ₹50,000; 85% of
net loss, max ₹25,000; once per lifetime) only if the fraud is reported to both the bank and NCRP/1930 within 5
calendar days. Banks must resolve domestic fraud complaints within 45 days.

8. Eligibility engine
A pure function with all rule constants in one module, so policy changes are a one-line edit with tests.
RULE_EFFECTIVE_FROM = 2027-01-01   COMP_MAX_LOSS = 50,000   COMP_RATE = 0.85
COMP_CAP = 25,000                   REPORT_WINDOW_DAYS = 5   BANK_RESOLVE_DAYS = 45
deadline_at = fraud_at + 5 days
status = first match of:
  fraud_at.date < RULE_EFFECTIVE_FROM                     → NOT_APPLICABLE_DATE
  prior_compensation                                       → NOT_APPLICABLE_PRIOR
  amount_total > COMP_MAX_LOSS                            → NOT_APPLICABLE_AMOUNT
  now > deadline_at and not (NCRP and bank before deadline) → WINDOW_MISSED
  NCRP (1930 or portal) and bank both reported before deadline → ELIGIBLE_COMPLETE
  otherwise                                                → ELIGIBLE_PENDING
max_compensation = min(round(0.85 × amount_total), 25,000) for ELIGIBLE_*; else 0

Same, all three reported on day 1 ELIGIBLE_COMPLETE · ₹15,300
₹40,000 ₹25,000 (cap)
₹60,000 NOT_APPLICABLE_AMOUNT
Fraud on 20 Dec 2026 NOT_APPLICABLE_DATE
Day 6, bank not reported WINDOW_MISSED
The engine produces an estimate. Final eligibility, including any negligence assessment, is the bank's decision; the UI
states this.

Figure 3. Fallback ladder — the demo can never stall on an AI call.
Single call: the image and the user's description are sent together; the model returns extraction ﬁelds, per-ﬁeld
conﬁdence, scam type, scam conﬁdence and a one-sentence summary as schema-constrained JSON. Unknown
values must be null — the prompt forbids guessing digits.
Validation: UPI UTR must be 12 digits; amount must be a positive integer; UPI IDs must match ^[\w.\-]
{2,256}@[a-zA-Z]{2,64}$; failures lower conﬁdence instead of rejecting.
Human in the loop: ﬁelds under 0.7 conﬁdence are highlighted, and nothing is generated until the user conﬁrms
every ﬁeld.
Keyword fallback classiﬁer: if the AI is unavailable, keywords map the description to a scam type (for example CBI,
customs, arrest, parcel → digital arrest) with conﬁdence 0.6.
Model choice: the model ID is conﬁguration, not code, so it can be swapped for a paid or self-hosted model (e.g.
Gemma) without changes.
10. Document generation

11. Security and privacy
11.1 Principles
Data minimisation: collect only what the three reports need; never OTP, PIN, password, card number or full
account number.
Purpose limitation and consent: data is used only to prepare the victim's reports; a consent line is shown before
upload.
User control: delete-my-case removes the case and evidence.
No silent action: Reclaim never submits to any government or bank system.

12. Reliability and demo safety
Demo clock: the RBI rule applies from 1 Jan 2027, but the event is in Oct 2026. In demo mode, now() starts at 12
Jan 2027, 8:01 PM IST and advances in real time; the stage can jump to day 4, day 30 or reset.
Oﬄine operation: ﬁxtures cover all sample screenshots; the full ﬂow runs with Wi-Fi oﬀ and no API key.
Seeded dashboard: about 12 fabricated cases across scam types and deadline states, so the bank view is never
empty.
Polling over websockets: a 2-second poll is simpler and survives reconnects.
Time handling: stored in UTC, displayed in Asia/Kolkata, tested around midnight boundaries.

15. Limitations and roadmap
No public API exists to submit NCRP complaints; ﬁling remains a user action by design.
Eligibility logic is based on secondary summaries of the RBI directions and must be checked against the oﬃcial
notiﬁcation; rules for non-commercial banks may diﬀer.
Extraction accuracy depends on screenshot quality; the conﬁrm step is mandatory for that reason.
Roadmap: more Indian languages, WhatsApp-native ﬂow, bank complaint-system integrations, insurer claim-pack
API, aggregated fraud-trend reports for banks and law enforcement.
