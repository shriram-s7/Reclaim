The First-5-Days Recovery Assistant for Cyber-Fraud Victims
1. Topic Title: Reclaim – First-5-Days Recovery Assistant for Cyber-Fraud Victims
2. Domain Selection: Cybersecurity & Digital Safety
3. Problem Statement
After a UPI or banking fraud, money is lost in the confused hours and days that follow. About 51% of UPI-fraud victims file no official
complaint and users call the reporting process complex (LocalCircles, 32,000+ responses). Delay is costly: Odisha recovered only ~2.7%
of ₹370 crore lost in 2023–25, with police citing late reporting (the420). Recovery needs three separate actions – a 1930 call, a full
complaint on cybercrime.gov.in using the 14-digit acknowledgement, and a written bank report – which most victims never complete.
From 1 Jan 2027, RBI's new directions compensate losses up to ₹50,000 (85%, max ₹25,000) only if reported to both 1930/NCRP and
the bank within 5 calendar days. Most victims will not know this rule.
4. Proposed Solution
Reclaim is a mobile-first web app and WhatsApp assistant (no install) that turns panic into a complete, correctly filed case:
 1
Read the evidence – victim uploads the UPI screenshot or bank SMS; Reclaim extracts UTR, amount, date-time, bank and
scammer's UPI ID.
 2
Act in the right order – a 1930 call script with every number asked, then a portal-ready NCRP complaint using the
acknowledgement, then a bank dispute letter.
 3
Never miss a deadline – live countdown to the 5-day compensation window, bank's 45-day resolution deadline, and RBI
Ombudsman escalation if the bank stalls.
 4
Stay safe – one evidence pack (PDF) for police and bank; warns against "recovery" agents who ask for money. Reclaim never
handles funds.
5. Technical Approach
Technology Stack: React PWA (mobile-first) · WhatsApp Business API (Twilio) · FastAPI · OCR (Tesseract / Google Vision) + regex parsers
for UTR, amount, UPI ID · LLM for scam-type tagging and complaint drafting · PostgreSQL · scheduled reminders (SMS/WhatsApp).
Key Features: screenshot-to-case extraction · guided 3-step filing · 5-day and 45-day deadline tracker · acknowledgement-number
vault · auto-generated complaint, bank letter and evidence PDF · Tamil/English · bank dashboard of cases and deadlines.
Prototype / MVP Feasibility: OCR pipeline, document generator, countdown tracker and dashboard are buildable in 24 hours. Live
demo: upload a sample fraud screenshot → fields fill in → countdown shows "Eligible: up to ₹25,000" → complaint, bank letter and
1930 script appear in seconds. No access to bank accounts – only evidence the victim chooses to share.
6. Business Approach
Target Customers / Users: Payers – banks (must process these claims under the new RBI rules), cyber-insurers and fintech/UPI apps.
Users – fraud victims (always free), police cyber cells.

7. Expected Impact
More victims complete all three reports on time · more cases eligible for RBI compensation · faster bank holds before money moves ·
cleaner evidence for police. Measured by: % of cases with both acknowledgements within 5 days, time from fraud to first report, and
% of eligible victims who receive compensation.
